# Changelog

## Unreleased

- First release.
- Bait tiers by biome: fish ignore bait from earlier biomes, and later-biome bait draws fish in more often and catches more starred fish.
- Fishing skill now raises hook and bite chance, shortens escapes, gives more line before it snaps, and goes further than vanilla on reel speed and stamina.
- Hooked fish reeled within grabbing distance are caught straight into your inventory.
- Catches can give a bonus fish, an extra star or your bait back, and award Fishing XP.
